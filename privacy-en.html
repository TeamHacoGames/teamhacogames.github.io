Privacy Policy

TeamHaco · Effective 7 October 2026

TeamHaco ("we", "us") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data in connection with the mobile games and related services we provide (the "Service").

1. Data We Collect and How

The Service does not require an account, and we do not ask you to enter your name, address or phone number. The following data may be generated or collected automatically while you use the Service.

We never collect or store payment instrument details such as card or bank account numbers. Payments are processed by Google Play, and Google handles that data.

2. Purposes of Processing

  • Providing the Service and maintaining game progress data
  • Processing in-app purchases, verifying purchase history, and handling refunds and support requests
  • Diagnosing crashes and errors, and improving service quality
  • Analysing gameplay statistics to adjust difficulty and balance and improve content (used in aggregated form that does not identify individuals)
  • Serving advertisements, including personalised advertising, and measuring ad performance
  • Preventing fraud and blocking unauthorised access
  • Responding to your enquiries

3. Retention Periods

We delete personal data without delay once the purpose of processing has been achieved, except where law requires a longer retention period.

  • Records of contracts and withdrawal of subscription: 5 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records of payment and supply of goods: 5 years (same Act)
  • Records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution: 3 years (same Act)
  • Service access and usage logs: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery; printed materials are shredded or incinerated.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose personal data to third parties beyond what is described in this Policy, except where you have given prior consent, or where disclosure is required by law or by a lawful request from an investigative authority following statutory procedure.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we have collected such data without the consent of a legal guardian, we delete it without delay.

We do not serve advertising targeted at children and configure our ad settings accordingly.

8. Your Rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of the processing of your personal data. For a child under 14, a legal guardian may exercise these rights.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, contact us using the details in section 10. We will act without undue delay. We may be unable to delete data that we are legally required to retain.

9. Advertising Identifiers and Personalised Ads

We use Google AdMob to serve advertising in the Service, which may involve collecting your advertising identifier.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent dialog appears when you first launch the app, asking whether you agree to data processing for advertising purposes. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time via "Privacy settings" in the in-game settings screen.

Resetting your advertising ID on Android

Settings → Google → All services → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation

Menu names and paths vary by device manufacturer and OS version.

Opting out of personalised advertising does not remove ads; you will simply see ads that are less relevant to you.
